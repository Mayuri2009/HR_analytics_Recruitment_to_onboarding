# Recruitment-to-Onboarding Process Transformation

A business analysis portfolio project: redesigning an HR recruitment and onboarding process from manpower requisition to the 90-day check-in.

> Note: Apex Industrial Services is a fictional company. All figures, baselines and sample data are assumed for illustration.

## The problem
Hiring takes 48 days on average, 22% of offers are lost, and only 55% of new joiners are ready on Day 1 (assumed baseline).

## Target outcomes
Time to fill 48 to 30 days, offer acceptance 78% to 88%, Day-1 readiness 55% to 95%, 90-day attrition 10% to 7%.

## Skills demonstrated
Requirements gathering, BRD and FSD writing, BPMN As-Is / To-Be mapping, gap and root cause analysis, user stories and acceptance criteria, source-to-target mapping, UAT planning and sign-off, KPI and reporting design, Excel.
